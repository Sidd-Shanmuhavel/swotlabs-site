# Virtual Customer Agent — Automated Email Triage, Data Extraction, and Case Creation

## Part One — Business Overview (for all readers)

The Virtual Customer Agent (VCA) handles the customer email arriving at an electricity network operator's connections team. We built it for UK Power Networks and its Connections Gateway Team, who field requests from customers connecting something new to the grid: a building, an EV charger, a solar array. Those requests arrive as email, usually with an application form attached. The team used to read, sort and process each one by hand.

VCA takes the front line of that work. It reads an incoming email and decides which of four things it is: a Job that creates new work, a Query about the status of existing work, a No-Further-Action email such as an autoreply or a thank-you, or an LCT Service Crew Job for low-carbon technology. For a Job, VCA opens the attached forms and pulls out the detail, including the customer's name and contact details, the site address, the connection type and the electrical loads, across dozens of fields. It matches the customer against existing records, opens a populated case in Salesforce, and attaches the original email to it.

Staff no longer read every email, decide what it is, retype the form and open the case. They spend their time on the requests that need judgement. When someone spots a wrong classification, VCA records the correction and feeds it back into the training set for the next model. It also tracks its own accuracy and publishes it on dashboards, so the team can see where it is weak.

## Part Two — How It Works End to End (for semi-technical readers)

VCA is a set of pipelines running on Databricks to a schedule through working hours. An email passes through four stages.

**Import and cleaning.** VCA connects to the team's Outlook mailbox over Microsoft's Graph API and pulls in new email with its attachments. It then strips the boilerplate footers, confidentiality notices and social-media links that pad out business email, leaving the content someone wrote.

**Classification.** Two methods decide what an email is. Hand-written rules catch the cases with a reliable signal, such as a sender or a subject line that always means one category. An AI model classifies whatever the rules leave, and VCA combines the two verdicts.

**Extraction**, for the emails that turn out to be jobs. The attached forms arrive as PDFs, Word documents and scanned images, so three readers work on them together: a fast text reader, a structured form-field reader, and a vision model that looks at the page the way a person does. Each reader is strong on different documents, so VCA reconciles their outputs into one answer per field.

**Matching and case creation.** VCA scores the extracted customer and site details against existing Salesforce accounts on address similarity, picks the closest account, and opens a Salesforce case with the fields filled and the email attached.

Supporting work runs alongside. VCA labels emails and files them in the right Outlook folders, records the misclassifications people catch, and feeds dashboards with the metrics. YAML configuration lets the same code run in development, pre-production and production against different endpoints and credentials, and Azure DevOps deploys and tests the platform.

## Part Three — Deep Technical Reference (for engineers)

### Domain and components

VCA classifies and routes incoming customer emails, extracts structured data from attachments (primarily electrical connection application forms), and creates Salesforce cases. It integrates Microsoft Outlook (Graph API), Azure OpenAI, Salesforce REST API, Azure Document Intelligence, and Databricks. The codebase splits into four major components: `vca_core` (about 11,955 lines of shared utilities), `vca_jobs` (about 23,718 lines of job orchestration), `deltaquality` (a data-quality framework), and `azure_pipeline` (CI/CD). The four email categories are job, query, nfa (no further action), and lct-job.

### `vca_core` — shared functionality

Email classification lives in `vca_core/email_classification/`: `llm_classification.py` (`LLMClassification` using Azure OpenAI GPT-4o Mini with async batch processing, rate limiting at 2000 requests per minute over a 60-second window, batches of twenty, temperature 0.1), `rule_classification.py` (`RuleClassification` applying regex rules with AND/OR logic chains over sender, subject, body, and form checks such as `subject_contains`, `latest_body_contains`, `sender`, `from`, `any_form_netcall`, and `any_form_salesforce`), and `classification_utils.py` (batch helpers including `process_email_batch()` and `truncate_email()` keeping tokens under 1250).

Entity extraction lives in `vca_core/entity_extraction/`: `entity_extraction_orchestrator.py` (the `EntityExtractionOrchestrator` consolidating PyMuPDF, Azure Document Intelligence, and GPT-4o Multimodal results by priority, with `consolidate_by_page()`, `consolidate_to_named_fields()`, and `is_address_json_complete()`), `llm_client.py` (a wrapper supporting retrieval-augmented generation via FAISS, direct queries, and multimodal queries with GPT-4o and GPT-4.1 and text-embedding-3-large), `document_intelligence_extraction.py`, `multimodal_extraction.py` (GPT-4o Vision processing pages with adjacent-page context), `pymupdf_extraction.py`, `docx_extraction.py` (using docx2python with token delimiters), `extraction_utils.py`, and the Salesforce field helpers `sf_field_processors.py` and `sf_field_utils.py`.

Account matching lives in `vca_core/accounts_matching/`: `address_matcher.py` computes address similarity as a weighted combination of 30 percent Jaro string similarity and 70 percent FAISS cosine embedding similarity; `embedding_generator.py` builds FAISS embeddings; `address_processor.py` normalises address strings. Data cleaning lives in `vca_core/data_cleaning/` (`dataframe_cleaning.py` with PySpark UDFs `clean_phone_udf`, `clean_email_udf`, `clean_postcode_udf`, `clean_date_string_udf`, and helpers `filter_by_max_col()` and `select_single_entry_for_window()`; `string_cleaning.py`). The top-level `llm_cleaner.py` defines `LLMCleaner`, async batch removal of boilerplate (confidentiality disclaimers, social links, external notices), rate-limited to 2000 per minute, up to ten concurrent runs, batches of fifty lines.

Machine-learning modelling lives in `vca_core/modelling/`: `models.py` (Random Forest, LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost, Logistic Regression, KNN, Naive Bayes, TensorFlow Sequential networks, a `ScikitModel` typing Protocol), `hyperopt.py` (Optuna optimisation), `metrics.py` (custom precision/recall/F1 scorers), and `mlflow.py` (experiment tracking, model registry, signatures, comparison). Outlook integration lives in `vca_core/outlook/` (`base_outlook_connector.py` with MSAL OAuth2, `email_connector.py`, `attachment_connector.py`, `email_mover.py`). Salesforce integration lives in `vca_core/salesforce/` (`base_api_connector.py` with OAuth2 client-credentials flow and `api_connector.py`'s `SalesforceApiConnector` using dynamic `__getattr__()` method generation for batch operations). Attachment processing's `entity_extraction_utils.py` identifies form types and extracts values. Utilities in `vca_core/utils/` include `config.py` (`!include` YAML), `async_utils.py` (a token-bucket `RateLimiter`), `database.py` (Delta operations), `retry.py` (`retry_factory()` with exponential backoff), plus logging, decorators, string and dict manipulation, and custom exceptions (`OutlookError`, `SalesforceError`, `NotRecognisedModelException`). Spark UDFs live in `spark_functions.py` (`length_without_spaces()`, `combine_ors()`, `fill_with_a1_details_unless_null()`).

### `vca_jobs` — orchestration

Classification jobs (`vca_jobs/classification/`): `email_classification_orchestrator.py` (`EmailClassification`, coordinating and merging LLM and rule results, excluding rule-matched emails from the LLM pass), `llm_classification.py` (`LLMEmailClassificationJob`), `rule_classification.py` (`RuleClassificationJob`), `model_training.py` (`ModelTrainingJob`, training scikit-learn models on labelled embeddings with Optuna and MLflow logging), `email_embeddings.py` (`EmailEmbeddingsJob`, text-embedding-3-large with 100 dimensions for subject and 800 for content), and `form_classifier.py` (`FormClassificationJob`, identifying form types from attachments).

Entity-extraction jobs (`vca_jobs/entity_extraction/`): an orchestrator `EntityExtractionJob` plus per-method jobs `PyMuPDFExtractionJob`, `DocumentIntelligenceJob`, `MultimodalExtractionJob` (with ±1 page buffering), `DocxExtractionJob`, an `ExtractionDataPrepJob` filtering documents to extract, and a `SalesforceEntityValuesJob` post-processing for case creation.

Outlook jobs (`vca_jobs/outlook_jobs/`): `EmailImport` (three strategies — inbox and misclassification folders at ten-minute deltas, jobs folder at a three-day lookback), `EmailLabelling` (applying Outlook categories such as VCA - Job, VCA - Query, VCA - NFA, VCA - LCT Job, Reviewed - Correct, Misclassified), `EmailMover` (routing to folders 0 - JOBS, 1 - QUERIES, 2 - NFA, 4 - LCT SC JOBS, and completion folders), `AttachmentImport` (batches of ten), `EmailEMLImport` (EML export for Salesforce with 200 MB partitions), and `modify_job_email.py`.

Salesforce jobs (`vca_jobs/salesforce_jobs/`): `SalseforceCreateCaseJob` (mapping fields such as JourneyType, ServiceType, QuotationType, and ServiceCompletionDate, validating mandatory customer fields FirstName, LastName, Email, Phone, Street1, City, and PostalCode, and matching site addresses with lower and upper thresholds of 0.8 and 0.9), `AttachCaseEMLJob`, `MatchAccountJob`, `RetrieveAccountJob`, and `SelectAccountJob`.

Misclassification tracking (`vca_jobs/misclassification/`): `LabelsCorrectorJob`, `TagChangesTrackerJob`, `TrueLabelsTrackerJob`, and `PredLabelsTrackerJob`. Data engineering (`vca_jobs/data_engineering/`): `EmailCleanerJob`, `JsonToTableJob`, `CleanUpJob` (180-day default and 30-day short retention with vacuuming), `resource_setup.py`, `alert.py`, and `exit_job_vals.py`. Dashboard jobs (`vca_jobs/dashboard_jobs/`): `DashboardTableJob`, `ModelMetricsJob`, and `SalesforceDashboardJob`. A `BaseJob` abstract class and a `CreateJob` factory underpin all of these, alongside testing decorators `@test_table_exact_match`, `@test_dq_table_outputs`, `@test_email_labler`, and `@test_email_mover`.

### Models, frameworks, and providers

Classification uses Azure OpenAI GPT-4o Mini (`vca-gpt-4o-mini`, API version 2024-12-01-preview); entity extraction uses GPT-4.1 (`vca-gpt-4.1`) for multimodal vision; embeddings use text-embedding-3-large (`vca-text-embedding-3-large`, API version 2023-05-15); all at temperature 0.1. Frameworks include LangChain and LangChain-OpenAI (chains, prompts, RAG with FAISS), scikit-learn, LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost, TensorFlow/Keras, PySpark 3.5.4 with Delta Lake, FAISS-CPU for vectors, PyMuPDF and PyPDF2 for PDFs, Azure Document Intelligence, docx2python and python-docx, aiohttp for async HTTP, Ray for distributed compute, Optuna for hyperparameter tuning, MLflow 2.15.1 for the model registry, MSAL for Outlook auth, BeautifulSoup, RapidFuzz, and tiktoken. Providers span Microsoft Azure (OpenAI, Document Intelligence, KeyVault, Databricks), Microsoft Graph (Outlook), Salesforce REST, and Azure DevOps.

### Form types and consolidation

Supported form types include G99_ENA_App_Form (pages 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 16, 28 with over fifty fields), Projects_App_Form (pages 2–6 with over forty fields), Projects_App_Form_v3, and Multiple_G98_Form. The four extraction methods (PyMuPDF text, Document Intelligence structured, GPT-4o Multimodal vision, DOCX parsing) are consolidated by priority (PyMuPDF over Multimodal over Document Intelligence) with majority voting, JSON address-object handling, address completeness validation (all lines required), and incomplete-JSON detection.

### Data quality

The `deltaquality` module implements Databricks Labs DQX expectations: `column_values_not_all_equal_to_value()` (detecting null injection or extraction failure where a whole column collapses to one value) and `is_not_in_list()`, registered via `@register_rule()` and attached to jobs through `@test_dq_table_outputs`, configured in `vca_jobs/config/dataquality_rules.yaml`.

### Inputs, outputs, and Delta tables

Email input arrives as JSON from Graph API (sender, subject, body, receivedDateTime, folder, attachment names). Form input is PDF, DOCX, or image. Classification input concatenates subject, latest body, the email thread, and attachment names. Salesforce input combines entity values, email metadata, and account match results. Outputs are classification labels with reasoning, per-field entity JSON, Salesforce case identifiers, and aggregated dashboard metrics. Key Delta tables include `sr_emails_inbox`, `br_success_attachments`, and `sr_attachment_form_type` (inputs); `sr_emails_llm_classified`, `sr_emails_rule_classified`, and `sr_emails_clean_body_classified` (classification); `sr_extraction_documents` and the per-method extraction tables (`sr_pymupdf_entity_extraction`, `sr_doc_int_entity_extraction`, `sr_multimodal_entity_extraction`, `sr_docx_entity_extraction`); the Salesforce tables `sr_salesforce_created_cases`, `sr_salesforce_case_attachments`, and `sr_salesforce_entity_values`; the audit tables `sr_runtime_audit_stats` and `sr_email_tag_status`; and the dashboard outputs `go_emails_metrics`, `go_model_metrics`, and `go_salesforce_viz`.

### Orchestration and scheduling

Jobs are instantiated through the `CreateJob` factory; the `BaseJob` base class supplies Spark, dbutils, config loading, and logging via `add_logger_to_init()`. The Databricks bundle defines five pipelines: `vca_pipeline` (main classification, every 30 minutes, Monday to Friday 09:00–18:00 UTC), `misclassification_intervention` (reprocessing, every 30 minutes, 10:00–18:00), `model_training`, `salesforce_email_processing` (case creation, every 30 minutes, 10:00–18:00), and `cleanup_pipeline`. The `notebooks/run_job.py` entry point offers environment and job dropdowns with parameter injection.

### Deployment and environments

Deployment uses Databricks Asset Bundles (`databricks bundle deploy -t <env>`) across dev, pre_prod, and prod, each binding distinct Azure OpenAI endpoints, Salesforce instances (sandbox versus production, the production mailbox being connections.gateway@ukpowernetworks.co.uk), Document Intelligence endpoints, catalogs, and secret scopes (`keyvault-managed-aipoc` for dev, `keyvault-managed-vca` for pre_prod and prod). Secrets referenced include the OpenAI API key, Salesforce client secret and password, Document Intelligence API key, and the Outlook Graph API key, all from Azure KeyVault via `dbutils.secrets.get`. The Azure DevOps pipeline (`azure_pipeline/cicd.yaml` with templates) runs build (pytest, coverage, diff-cover, pre-commit linting), staging, and release stages on environment-specific self-hosted agents. Documentation is generated with Sphinx and AutoAPI (`docs/conf.py` and `docs/index.rst`) covering the three packages, mocking heavy dependencies, and producing both HTML and PDF.
